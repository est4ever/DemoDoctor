## Scientific findings and limits

The three scorable retrospective held-outs are EP003, EP005 and EP013. EP006
has disputed reference evidence; EP010 is development; PILOT has no independent
binary reference. The pipeline matches two definitive reference outcomes and
abstains once. Muse-only produces EP005's wrong success. This is **not** a new
unseen-video study: these recordings have already been inspected in development.

EP013 has a supported outside-target 2D sample; its cause and later recovery
remain unverified. EP005 has a saved plausible flat-orientation hypothesis,
not a measured pose. No independently verified physical causal mechanism is
scored; abstention is not correct failure identification. Per-statement semantic
false-claim count is not established, even though cited IDs validate.

The retained held-out track caches contain 120 unique frames per recording,
not full-video tracking. Some frozen runs processed additional windows whose
observation payloads are not distributed. PILOT retains 632/737 frames and its
105-frame gap; its packaged track export has no mask payloads. These coverage
counts are not tracking identity continuity, segmentation accuracy or recall.

For new videos, approved physical orientation, expected counts, calibrated
final containment and holding-sensitive release measurements are still absent
unless independently supplied by a future supported input. The current live
assessment reports those limits rather than creating physical facts. Muse sees
selected context images plus tracking evidence, not an unrestricted analysis
of every video frame. Multi-camera visibility alone proves neither identity
nor 3D geometry.
