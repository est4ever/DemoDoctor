## Method boundaries

- Criteria states use only saved pipeline artifacts. `supported`/`contradicted` require existing citations; otherwise they are demoted to `unverified`. Missing evidence is never scored as failure.
- `criteria_completion` is a new, stricter view, not a replacement of the frozen pipeline verdict. It abstains on ep003 because the declared orientation criterion is unmeasured.
- Orientation is declared required for BotFails from the dataset's task-level definition (the 'wrong way' anomaly class); no per-episode band or label is an input.
- Mechanism and physical-assertion matching use transparent keyword rules (see `CATEGORY_TERMS`, `PHYSICAL_TERMS`); they can miss paraphrases and should be read alongside the quoted text.
- EP010 is a development episode; EP003/005/006/013 are held-out; PILOT has no outcome reference. EP006 is excluded from scoring because its references conflict.
